    CLEAR STEPS. NO SURPRISE CHARGES.

    Pay for the service, not for a promise.

    Founder review · ₹499 once

    1. Complete founder details and deck.
    2. Invited founders submit without paying.
    3. Open applicants review the fee, then pay via Razorpay.
    4. A valid invited submission, or an open submission with verified payment, produces the application ticket.

    The fee pays for review. It does not buy admission, investment or funding. Already applied? Check status, do not pay again.

    Investor directory · ₹999 monthly access

    1. Onboarded approved profiles and interest stay free.
    2. Choose the separate wider funding directory directly.
    3. Review coverage, access duration and payment/refund terms.
    4. Pay via Razorpay; confirmed payment unlocks that directory.

    No interest submission required first. Renewal method and final terms remain pending. No checkout is active.

    What if payment fails, is cancelled, or looks uncertain?

    No duplicate charge. Failed/cancelled founder payment does not create a paid application; failed/cancelled directory payment leaves it locked while free browsing remains available. If money was debited or status is pending, check the existing payment before another attempt. A checkout success message alone is not final confirmation.
