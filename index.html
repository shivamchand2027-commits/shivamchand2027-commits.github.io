Frequently asked questions

Who gets in?

A small minority. Most applications are declined. The Investment Committee looks for real traction, founders who have already done the work, and a credible reason to be in the room. Founders who treat access as a formality are not admitted.

What is Adya Capital?

An invitation-only founder network and funding desk. Anyone may apply - an invitation code is the fast lane, not a requirement. No money buys admission: open applications carry a one-time Rs 499 review fee, and invited founders apply free.

Why is access by invitation only?

Access is a filter, not a feature. It keeps the room small and serious. Every admitted member may nominate up to 5 founders, and their name stands behind each nomination, so nominations are made carefully.

What happens after I apply?

The Investment Committee reviews your application and emails you when there is a decision. You can also check your status anytime with your email and ticket. Approved founders receive a direct line to the Managing Partner. Declined founders may reapply only with materially stronger traction.

How should I prepare my deck?

Aim for 10 to 15 slides covering the problem, solution, market, traction, business model, team and the ask. Share a Drive link with access set to Anyone with the link, or upload a PDF up to 10 MB.

Is there a fee?

Founders applying with an invitation code pay nothing. Open applications (without a code) carry a one-time Rs 499 review fee - it pays for the Committee's time, not for admission, and most applicants are still declined.
